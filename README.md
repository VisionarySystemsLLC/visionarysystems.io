# visionarysystems.io

The company site for Visionary Systems. Plain HTML and CSS, generated by a short Python script, served by GitHub Pages from the `docs/` folder.

## Layout

```
content/essays/   one text file per Calibration Series piece (NN-slug.md)
src/style.css     the whole design system (tokens at the top)
src/assets/       favicons, the home-screen icon, link-preview images (og/), self-hosted fonts (fonts/)
build.py          turns content/ and src/ into docs/
tools/og.py       renders the link-preview images and the Reddit assets
brand/            Reddit banner and avatar, rendered from the site's own mark and type
docs/             the published site (GitHub Pages serves this folder)
```

The build also writes `docs/404.html` (served for any missing address), `docs/robots.txt`, and `docs/sitemap.xml`.

## Adding an effortpost to The Calibration Series

1. Create `content/essays/05-your-slug.md` (the number sets the order; the slug becomes the page URL).
2. Put four header lines at the top, then a line of three dashes, then the essay with a blank line between paragraphs:

   ```
   title: The title as posted
   date: December 1, 2026
   link: https://www.reddit.com/r/cognitiveTesting/comments/...
   summary: One or two sentences for the archive list.
   ---

   First paragraph.

   Second paragraph.
   ```

   Inline HTML is fine inside a paragraph, for example a link.
3. Run `python3 tools/og.py` to render the piece's link-preview image (needs Playwright with Chromium), then `python3 build.py`, then commit `content/`, `src/assets/og/`, and `docs/` and push to `main`. The page, the archive entry, and the "n of N" counts update themselves.

Or send the posted text and permalink to Claude, which does the same steps and pushes.

## Everything else

- Site copy lives in `build.py` (home, the Scan, The Visionary, About). Edit there, rebuild, push.
- Widths are tokens at the top of `src/style.css`: `--wide` (the whole container), `--rail` (the heading rail on interior sections), `--measure` (prose inside the content column) and `--measure-essay` (essay pages). Paragraphs are justified and hyphenated from 760px up and left-aligned on phones. The rail layout switches on at 1000px; below that, headings stack above their content.
- Two accent colors: `--gold` (the mark, links, actions, numbers) and `--ion` (labels, thresholds, the far end of every gradient). Glows use `--glow` and `--glow-ion`.
- Interior pages are built from `section(rail(...), content)` in `build.py`: the first argument is the heading and its small label, the second is everything beside it.
- There is no signup form on purpose. Ghost collects subscribers once the publication launches in March 2027; until then the publication page points people to the Reddit account and the contact address. The Calibration Series continues as the precursor until launch.
- The calibration numbers (47 started, 8 finished, 6 reached The Zenith) are from the September note. Update them in `build.py` when the next note publishes.
- `SERIES_TOTAL` in `build.py` is the planned length of The Calibration Series (8). Every "n of 8" on the site reads it; the published count comes from the files in `content/essays/`.
- The design scales with the screen: the root font size grows from 16px at laptop width to 21px on wide monitors, and everything is measured in rem, so proportions and line lengths hold from a phone to an ultrawide. There is also a print stylesheet for the essays.
- Spectral (300 for page titles, 400, 500, 600) and Spectral SC are self-hosted from `src/assets/fonts/` (OFL license alongside), so nothing loads from Google.
- Page heads are two columns: eyebrow, title and lede on the left; a figure or facts on the right (`page_head()` in `build.py` takes the right-hand block as its last argument).
- Essay pages have a paper theme (the "Read on paper" button), remembered per browser in `localStorage`. The tokens for it are under `:root[data-theme="paper"]` in the stylesheet. Other pages stay navy.
- Motion is confined to the hero (a few stars twinkle, a spark rides the mark's ring, the mark breathes) and the reading-progress line on essays, and all of it switches off under the reader's reduced-motion setting.
- The profile figure on the Scan page is illustrative (`profile_figure()` in `build.py` holds the five values and their error widths). The observer figure is `observer()`.
- "Launching March 2027" framing appears in three places in `build.py` (home kicker and paragraph, publication page, first-issue note). Change them together.
- GitHub Pages settings: source is the `main` branch, folder `/docs`, custom domain `visionarysystems.io`. `docs/CNAME` is written by the build, so never delete it.
- DNS at the registrar: four `A` records on the apex pointing to 185.199.108.153, 185.199.109.153, 185.199.110.153, 185.199.111.153, and a `CNAME` for `www` pointing to `visionarysystemsllc.github.io`.
