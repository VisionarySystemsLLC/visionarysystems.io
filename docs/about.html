One conviction

The company builds one instrument, the Visionary Psychometric Scan, and publishes one periodical, The Visionary. Both run on the same conviction: measurement earns trust by showing its error bars, treating a score of 105 with the same rigor as a score of 130, and telling people plainly what a result cannot say about them.

The founder

The founder goes by Morgan. He came to psychometrics the way most of its enthusiasts do, by finding the testing communities online in early 2025 and reading past the point of casual interest. The Scan began as a question about what the standard batteries leave out, particularly for people with uneven profiles, and became an instrument when no existing test answered it.

How this is made

The Scan's items were built with AI assistance and vetted by hand; they are original to the instrument and drawn from no existing test. The writing is drafted with an AI assistant as well. The arguments, the verification, and the decisions are the founder's, and anything on this site that can be checked is meant to be.
