Visionary Systems is a small psychometrics observatory in Greenville, South Carolina. It builds the Visionary Psychometric Scan, an untimed reasoning instrument now in beta calibration, and it publishes The Visionary, a publication written from inside that calibration.

The Visionary Psychometric Scan

Beta 2.0, in calibration since July 2026

36 items, untimed
Eight options each, paper and pencil allowed, adaptive after the first tier
Five coordinates
Fluid, crystallized, visuospatial, quantitative, and integrative reasoning
Three tiers
The Gate, The Bridge, and The Zenith, each harder than the last

The Scan measures reasoning in a high and narrow band and says so. It sets no clock, requires no outside knowledge, and reports the shape of your reasoning rather than a single number. Most people who start it will not clear The Gate, and the report is written with that in mind: the floor of the instrument is not the floor of the person.
