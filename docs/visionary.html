The publication

The Visionary

Launching March 2027

A publication on psychometrics, written from inside an instrument's calibration.

Most writing about intelligence testing is written by people who have never had to norm anything. The Visionary is written by someone who is in the middle of it, with the dropout tables and the item statistics on the desk. It covers what tests can and can't tell you, which parts of the field's orthodoxy survive contact with data, and what the Scan's own calibration turns up as it goes.

The Visionary launches in March 2027. The Calibration Series is its precursor: one piece a month about building and calibrating the Scan, posted first on r/cognitiveTesting, where the arguments get tested in the comments, and archived here. The series runs until launch, and the launch builds on it.
